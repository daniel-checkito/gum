FAQ

How much caffeine is in one piece?

52 to 60 mg depending on the flavor: Hotfix 60 mg, Ship It and Demo Day 52 mg. The number is on the front of every tin.

How many can I have per day?

Max 3 pieces a day. Count your coffee, mate and energy drinks too. Not recommended for children and pregnant women.

Is it really sugar-free?

Yes. It is sweetened with xylitol. Like all xylitol products, too much can have a laxative effect, and xylitol is toxic to dogs.

When does it ship?

With the first batch. Reserve now and we email you once before it ships. No payment until then.

Can I get it for my event or team?

Yes. Event hosts get a host box, teams get a team box with invoice. Pick your type in the form below.

Will it make me 10x?

No. It's gum with caffeine. We don't promise flow states, we promise the number on the front.
