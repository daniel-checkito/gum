The GUMMIT story

Long build days in Berlin. The coffee in the kitchen is cold again and the vending machine only has sugar. So we started GUMMIT: a gum with caffeine, zero sugar and the dose printed on the front.

First stop: the Luma events, hackathons and coworkings where Berlin builds stuff.

FAQ

What exactly is it?

Sugar-free chewing gum with caffeine. Each piece has 52 to 60 mg caffeine depending on the flavor. 8 pieces come in one metal tin.

How many can I have per day?

Max 3 pieces a day. Count your coffee, mate and energy drinks too. Not recommended for children and pregnant women.

How does reserving work?

Pick flavor and pack, leave your email. No payment now. We email you once before the first batch ships, then you decide.

Where do you ship?

From Berlin to Germany, Austria and Switzerland.

Anything to watch out for?

Xylitol can have a laxative effect if you overdo it, and it is toxic to dogs.

Will it make me 10x?

No. It's gum with caffeine. The number on the front is the whole pitch.
